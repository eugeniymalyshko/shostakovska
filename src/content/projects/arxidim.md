description: ARXIDIM is a bureau working with buildings and their documentation. For its visual identity, I started with a floor plan and reduced it to a few lines. I wanted the mark to read as both a plan and a symbol. The geometry of the mark became the grid for the rest of the identity. I used it to place type and organise information across posters and business cards. The palette moves between black and white and a sharper blue and white, while the layouts follow the same grid.
