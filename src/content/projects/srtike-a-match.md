description: Strike a Match, Ignite the Flame is a series of visual experiments built around the image of a match, fire, and the moment of ignition. The project started from a simple phrase and gradually developed through different techniques — collage, photography, drawing, digital manipulation, and typography. Rather than creating one fixed visual identity, I used the same sentence as a starting point for multiple interpretations. Across the series, familiar objects are transformed into strange, playful forms, while typography becomes part of the image itself — stretched, interrupted, repeated, or embedded into the composition.
