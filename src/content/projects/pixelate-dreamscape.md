description: Pixelate Dreamscape is a commonplace book exploring the early internet, personal computers and the visual culture surrounding them. The publication combines found texts, archival imagery, early interfaces, handwritten notes and personal annotations. Instead of constructing a chronological history of technology, I used the book as a space for collecting, interrupting and reconnecting information. Printed and digital material continuously overlap, turning research into a visual archive somewhere between technological history, personal memory and the aesthetics of the early web.
